# Data attribution

External benchmark/viewer material uses **Manuel Bassek, Robert Rein, Hendrik Weber and Daniel Memmert, “An integrated dataset of spatiotemporal and event data in elite soccer.”** Figshare, https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.28196177.v1. Dataset description: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-04505-y. Licence: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Our changes are parsed/centred coordinates converted for display, thinned pitch samples, label mapping, synthetic observation-dropout scenarios, learned predictions and derived official-scorer benchmark measurements. The benchmark is development evidence on consumed external matches, not a target-match score. The authors/provider do not endorse this application or its predictions. Preserve this attribution with any permitted external-data demonstration.

Synthetic animation and authored demonstration labels are generated by this project. They are software fixtures, not match annotations or accuracy measurements. Restricted competition recordings, tracking, predictions and private viewer material are excluded from any public-safe demonstration and remain subject to their separate terms.
